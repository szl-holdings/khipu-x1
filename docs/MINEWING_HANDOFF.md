# Minewing One-Prototype Handoff

## Current stage

Concept, system architecture and software-reference implementation. No custom
PCB, production enclosure, FPGA bitstream or custom ASIC currently exists.

## Requested engagement

Support initial architecture through delivery of **one complete owner engineering
prototype**. Quote mass production separately only if requested later.

## Minewing workstream

- recommend two viable compute/FPGA/security architectures;
- identify whether FPGA engineering is internal or performed by a named partner;
- component selection, lifecycle and preliminary BOM;
- schematic and PCB design;
- memory, power, signal-integrity, thermal and RF engineering;
- TPM/secure-element and independent RC1 MCU integration;
- board-management/BSP firmware and bring-up;
- prototype enclosure and mechanical integration;
- factory programming/provisioning/test approach;
- assembly, bench validation and shipment of one working prototype.

## SZL workstream

- A11oy software and Reality Protocol;
- KIDS, simulator, compiler/runtime direction and `.khipu` format;
- models, weights, policy, receipt semantics and evaluation;
- proprietary algorithms, source and cloud control plane.

## Required quote breakdown

1. Phase 0 feasibility/architecture.
2. FPGA design labor.
3. PCB/electrical NRE.
4. Mechanical/enclosure NRE.
5. Firmware/BSP/RC1 labor.
6. Components, PCB fabrication and assembly.
7. Bring-up, test and shipping.
8. Total all-in price for exactly one prototype.
9. Calendar schedule and long-lead risks.
10. Optional sponsorship, credit or deferred-NRE proposal stated separately.

## Deliverables to SZL

Project-specific schematics, PCB design source and Gerbers, BOM/AVL, mechanical
CAD, board-management/RC1 firmware source created for the project, FPGA
source/constraints/build scripts created for the project, test procedures,
machine-readable test results and reproducibility notes, subject to disclosed
background IP and third-party licenses.

## Exclusions

No ASIC tapeout, production tooling, production order, volume commitment,
exclusivity, equity, software/model transfer, patent transfer, publicity right
or endorsement is authorized.
